Event Overview

Advancing Maritime Prosperity Zones & the U.S. Maritime Action Plan

The U.S. Center for Maritime Innovation (USCMI) is hosting an Innovation Café and Workshop focused on identifying and evaluating regional maritime incubators and other existing federal, state, regional, and private sector initiatives that align with Maritime Prosperity Zones (MPZs) and support U.S. Maritime Action Plan (MAP) objectives.

Regional maritime incubators represent strategic frameworks designed to leverage existing port infrastructure, maritime services, and industrial ecosystems to drive economic growth, enhance operational resilience, and attract investment in emerging maritime technologies and business models. This event seeks to connect researchers, startups, technology developers, government agencies, investors, shipowners/operators, shipyards, and other maritime stakeholders around these priority topics.

How to Participate

Participation Roles

Three participation roles are available. Applicants may submit for one or multiple roles.

01

Presenter — Give a short overview of your regional maritime projects and initiatives during the Innovation Café. Note whether you are also interested in exhibiting.

02

Exhibitor — Provide static or dynamic displays on your regional maritime projects and initiatives. Note whether you are also interested in presenting.

03

General Attendee — Attend the exhibits and join the facilitated workshop. Through structured discussion, participants assess the structure, resources, governance, partnerships and outcomes of representative maritime regional innovation efforts and identify strengths, gaps, overlaps, barriers, lessons learned and best practices.

We are seeking submissions from stakeholders focused on identifying and evaluating past, ongoing, and planned efforts pertaining to Maritime Prosperity Zones and U.S. Maritime Action Plan objectives.
